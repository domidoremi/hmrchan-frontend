Electricity, at the right time.

(Scroll down)

Sobry operates in one of the most unstable markets there is. Prices fluctuate constantly, offers are hard to read, and consumers are left feeling like they’re reacting instead of choosing.
The challenge was clear: make a complex product instantly understandable and build trust in seconds.

Brand Appart partnered with Sobry to clarify the promise and translate it into a reassuring, structured brand and product experience. The design focuses on readability and guidance: easy-to-understand dynamic pricing, a price cap that protects users from spikes, and practical coaching that helps people act at the right moment.
‍

Sobry doesn’t try to beat the market.
Sobry gives control back turning price volatility into a simple routine, and uncertainty into informed decisions.
